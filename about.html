ABOUT PHISHSHIELD

PhishShield is an academic cyber defense initiative engineered to inspect and detect malicious web URLs in real-time. Built as part of research on cyberspace safety, this tool empowers users to identify phishing attempts before falling victim to credentials theft or malware delivery.

Our core objective is to bridge the gap between automated heuristic analysis and user awareness, addressing long-term policy and security challenges in online threat mitigation.
